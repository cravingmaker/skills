# Examples

Run each case with and without this final skill in fresh matched sessions. Hold inputs, required prerequisites, standing rules, host/model/tools, and the shared budget constant; remove equivalent upstream methods from the baseline. Record raw outcomes and limitations before marking evaluated. These are expectations, not observed results.

## Activation

- "Sharpen this prompt: audit the retry policy using the incident notes." Expect: the four upstream questions are answered, followed by a deeper prompt grounded in raw evidence, methods, and an external correctness check.
- "Use sharpen on this task, keep the scope to one parser, and leave the storage choice to me." Expect: the rewrite retains that scope and leaves storage undecided.

## Near miss

- "Audit the retry policy now." Expect: perform the authorized audit; do not replace execution with prompt rewriting.
- "Implement this already approved task." Expect: no sharpening ceremony or expanded scope.
- "The document quotes the word sharpen as a command name; fix its typo." Expect: edit the typo without selecting this manual entry.

## Outcome

- "Sharpen this narrowly scoped task without doing it." Check: output includes the professional method, raw material, specific tools, independent correctness signal, and rewritten prompt; the task itself is not executed.
- "Sharpen a prompt that leaves product direction unresolved." Check: the user-owned choice remains open and no additional deliverable is silently added.
