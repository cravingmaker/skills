# Examples

Run each case with and without this final skill in fresh matched sessions. Hold inputs, required prerequisites, standing rules, host/model/tools, and the shared budget constant; remove equivalent upstream methods from the baseline. Record raw outcomes and limitations before marking evaluated. These are expectations, not observed results.

## Activation

- "Bro. Say your last answer plainly." Expect: read the required parent and dispatch to repair-last-message mode, preserving meaning and uncertainty.
- "Bro, make the last read-only council findings understandable." Expect: repair the wording of the findings; start no fix loop or remote action.

## Near miss

- "The source says bro; quote that line exactly." Expect: preserve the quote without selecting this manual entry.
- "Rename the variable bro to brother." Expect: the scoped rename, without repairing the previous answer.
- "What does the word bro mean?" Expect: answer the vocabulary question without dispatching.

## Outcome

- "Bro, explain your draft PR body more plainly." Check: the parent repairs the last draft without filing, pushing, watching, or merging.
- "Bro, clarify the prepare-only result after a full-stop helper chain." Check: no-merge authority persists through the repair; no helper is started.
- "Bro, restate a claim that passed only static review." Check: facts and proof limits stay exact; a parent missing from the installed inventory is reported rather than improvised.
