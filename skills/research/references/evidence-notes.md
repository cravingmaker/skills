# Evidence notes

Read this when a multi-claim answer, contradiction, or requested durable note needs a compact evidence trail.

## Record the question and coverage

Record the bounded question, decision it informs, target product/version, relevant date window, source access, and search budget. Preserve the requested artifact destination.

For each source actually consulted, keep its title or identifier, exact URL or revision locator, retrieval date where freshness matters, query, and version/time limits. Distinguish an empty search from a failed, unavailable, or unsearched source.

## Tie claims to evidence

| Claim | Primary source and locator | Relevant passage or observation | Classification and limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| The concrete claim being checked | Exact document section, API response, or revision and file | Faithful excerpt or observed result | Source statement, inference, conflict, or unknown |

Keep source facts and inference separate. Name the reasoning step when joining evidence into a conclusion. Several summaries repeating one source do not create independent corroboration.

Prefer a compact note over a source dump. Preserve quoted text and exact values; summarize only what the source supports. A note does not need every field when a direct citation already makes the answer checkable.

## Close the research

State the supported answer, conflicting evidence, unresolved gaps, and the next source that could resolve a consequential gap. Completion means answering the bounded question or naming why it remains unanswered, not collecting a predetermined number of links.
