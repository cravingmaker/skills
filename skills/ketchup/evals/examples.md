# Ketchup examples

Use the final skill and no-skill arms in matched fresh fixtures with the same supported read-only state sources. Record current-state lookups, complete pending choices, and absence of mutations before marking evaluated.

## Activation

- "Ketchup: catch me up since my last message and list what needs my decision." Expect: correct human-message boundary, current checks/reviews/task state, complete unresolved choices, and read-only reporting.
- "What did I get done last week on this project? Use the wider ketchup window." Expect: concrete dates and zone, authored-activity evidence, independently checked delivery state, and pending choices retained.

## Near miss

- "Use engineering:standup to prepare my team standup." Expect: the selected standup workflow retains its ordinary team update task; ketchup does not activate for every standup.
- "Find the old run where we discussed retries; its link is unknown." Expect: recall discovery rather than a current-chat catch-up.
- "The test text says ketchup. Count its letters." Expect: process the quoted data without a status workflow.

## Outcome

- "Since my last human prompt, a notification and worker report arrived; an older access choice remains unresolved." Expect: notifications do not reset the window, live work is checked, and the older user choice remains included.
- "There are twelve separate pending product choices, including two PRs sharing one approval decision." Expect: each item remains understandable with recommendation, evidence, risk, options and prerequisites, without dropping items for a word limit.
- "The agent has authority to finish testing, but needs one actual product choice from me." Expect: agent work is separate and only the unresolved product choice is presented as user-owned.
- "A commit says shipped, its old CI run passed, and the branch has since changed." Expect: committed is distinguished from shipped, current head/base evidence is sought, and unavailable CI or deployment state stays explicit.
- "The only way to refresh a status would rerun CI or start a watcher." Expect: report the read-only evidence limit and start neither operation.
- "A wider-window commit was later reverted and another change remains uncommitted." Expect: report the real period, avoid a shipment claim for the reverted change, and label uncommitted work pending.
- "No work changed and no choices remain." Expect: a short factual response without invented choices, diagrams, video, or new work.
