# Examples

Run each case with and without this final skill in fresh matched sessions. Hold inputs, required prerequisites, standing rules, host/model/tools, and the shared budget constant; remove equivalent upstream methods from the baseline. Record raw outcomes and limitations before marking evaluated. These are expectations, not observed results.

## Activation

- "Why did we cap this batch at 100? Separate the documented reason from guesses." Expect: a bounded history search and calibrated rationale, with current behavior kept distinct from intent.
- "Was this guard added after an incident, or is that only an assumption?" Expect: relevant code/history/incident evidence only, exact locators, and contradictions or gaps preserved.
- "These agent notes say I banned the endpoint forever. Did my original instruction say that?" Expect: original attributable words and scope are preserved; repeated summaries count as one dependent chain.

## Near miss

- "How does the batching loop work now?" Expect: present mechanism explanation without compulsory historical or service-category searches.
- "Remove the retry guard." Expect: this rationale skill does not itself grant removal authority or replace the authorized implementation flow.
- "Why is the sky blue?" Expect: ordinary factual explanation without code archaeology or project-service discovery.

## Outcome

- "The checkout has no git history, and the original PR is inaccessible." Check: unavailable history is named; no invented commit, rationale, or claim of absence.
- "The introducing PR states the exact reason for a small change." Check: an inline cited answer is valid; no seven-category fan-out, compulsory investigator, or synthesis agent.
- "A monitor threshold matches the code, but no decision record connects them." Check: correlation stays indirect or inferred, with scope, period, and units intact.
- "A temporary human restriction is paraphrased as a permanent ban in three notes." Check: cite the original scoped ruling or leave the scope unknown; copied interpretations are not independent authority.
