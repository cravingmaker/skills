# Evidence synthesis prompt

Read this when reconciling several findings. It may be used inline; no separate synthesizer is required.

## Inputs

Question and code anchor: `{question_and_anchor}`.

Findings with locators, queries, time windows, contradictions, and access limits: `{findings}`.

Relevant sources not searched and why: `{coverage_limits}`.

Read the epistemics rules linked from SKILL.md before classifying claims.

## Reconcile the record

Read all findings. Merge overlapping references without treating copies as independent evidence. Check a consequential uncertain citation against the source when available.

For human rulings, preserve the original speaker, wording, and scope. Keep summaries labelled and leave the scope uncertain if the original is unavailable.

Show contradictions rather than forcing a winner. A plausible story does not close an evidence gap.

## Shape the answer

Adapt the structure to the question:

- **Question and target.** The bounded uncertainty, file/symbol anchors, and relevant version.
- **Found.** Direct statements with citations, and separately labelled supported conclusions with their evidence.
- **Inferred.** The reasoning chain and its uncertainty.
- **Competing hypotheses.** Evidence for, evidence against, and what remains missing.
- **Unknown.** Unanswered questions and concrete coverage limits.
- **Sources consulted.** Actual queries, locators, periods, and results. Label unavailable and unsearched sources honestly.
- **Confidence.** What is well established and which part remains uncertain.

Short direct answers can combine these into a paragraph. Preserve the confidence separation rather than printing every heading.

If the answer will inform an already authorized change, identify evidence-backed preserve/change/avoid/risk constraints. Do not convert an uncertain rationale into permission, a ban, or a repair task.

Done when each consequential claim is supported or calibrated, contradictions and relevant gaps remain visible, and another reader can check the cited record.
