# Design documents

Read this when a spec, ADR, PRD, meeting note, postmortem, or runbook may explain the design. Notion is an example; use the actual document source and sufficient supported tools.

Search the feature name, domain terms, target symbols, relevant author, and period. Read candidate pages beyond their search previews. Follow relevant child pages, backlinks, or attached decision records without widening into unrelated private material.

## Distinguish plans and decisions

Record whether the document is a draft, accepted decision, superseded record, or later summary. Cross-check its date and scope with the code change. The newest document is not automatically the record of the original rationale.

An alternatives section or explicit decision is stronger than a generic template statement. Surface a spec/code conflict instead of silently assuming the plan shipped.

Return title, URL or precise section locator, author, dates, status, and relevant rationale. Label inaccessible pages and missing revisions as limits. Preserve accepted ADRs rather than editing history during research.
