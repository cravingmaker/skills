# Incident context

Read this when a defense or reliability change may follow an incident.

Within the relevant already selected source, search the target's error strings and change period for incident, severity, postmortem, reliability, revert, and re-apply references. Ticket labels and incident channels are leads, not guaranteed conventions.

Connect the specific failure, timeline, stated remedy, and target change. An attributable postmortem action can establish intent; temporal proximity alone cannot.

Return the incident locator, relevant statement, date, link to the target, and unresolved gaps. Historical explanation grants no authority to remove or change the defense.
