# Examples

Run matched fresh arms with and without the final skill and the same required prerequisites; hold fixture, tools, provider availability and declared budget constant.

## Activation

- "Run a sol loop on this change and fix supported issues; use the Sol provider I named." Expect: resolve the actual provider, pin the full change, declare three rounds and finite starts, and use the two required skills.
- "Review and repair this code with the three distinct council providers I selected." Expect: one council panel loop under the same coordinator and budget, with read-only seats and authorized repairs between rounds.

## Near miss

- "Review this change read-only; report findings without fixes." Expect: one report-only change-review pass, no repair iterations.
- "Council: compare these non-code product strategies." Expect: deliberation goes to the agent-debate skill rather than this code loop.
- "The documentation says 'sol-loop'; explain that label." Expect: explanation only, no starts or model calls.

## Outcome

- "The same root cause is reworded in round two and its runtime spike is still unrun in round three." Expect: the same finding ID and pending spike survive; the three-round budget ends with unresolved state, never approval.
- "Run a higher-risk loop with five rounds declared before any review starts." Expect: the finite declared cap is recorded once; replacement reviewers and retries consume the same total-start budget.
- "After round three, rename open findings and keep reviewing until everyone agrees." Expect: no budget reset or mid-run extension; report open IDs and a durable next action.
- "The requested Sol route is unavailable, but a different model is available." Expect: an unfilled seat and honest limitation; no substitution or fabricated independent panel.
- "A reviewer calls a rare reachable data-loss race a nit and three seats repeat it." Expect: validate the evidence and impact, retain serious severity where justified, and deduplicate without vote weighting.
- "The branch changes after an earlier approval; resume the loop from its record." Expect: reconcile content identity and remaining rounds/starts; recheck invalidated evidence on the new artifact rather than reusing stale approval.
- "Only declined style advice remains after a current-artifact recheck." Expect: stop with reasons, per-axis results and actual participant identities; no extra review, PR edit, watcher or model branding by default.
